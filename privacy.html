About this site

Aurora Tracker is a real-time monitoring tool for Northern Lights enthusiasts. Our mission is to provide accurate, easy-to-read space weather data to help users predict and catch the Aurora Borealis.

Aurora Tracker is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information and use cookies when you visit our website.

This data is used solely to improve the user experience and monitor the technical performance of the service.
